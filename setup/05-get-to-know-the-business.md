# Step 7: get to know the business

Goal: short, accurate notes in `context/` so your proposals sound like them and are right about them. Work most of it out yourself; ask as little as possible.

`context/sales.md` and `context/proposals.md` already hold what they sell, their packages, how they deliver and how they start with new clients (step 3). Don't ask any of that again: use it.

## 7.1 Work it out first

With the connections from steps 5 and 6, quietly gather:
- From their **sent email** (last 2 to 3 weeks): who they deal with most, their clients, how they write.
- From **calls**: how they talk about their work, and what clients ask most.
- From their **website**: what the business does, its story, its proof (reviews, case studies, awards).

Don't present guesses as facts. Use them to skip questions.

## 7.2 Learn how they write

Ask once:

> Can I read 15 or so of your recent sent emails, and any proposals you've sent, to learn how you write? Then your proposals will sound like you. I'll keep notes on your style, not copies.

If yes, follow `.claude/skills/write-like-me` in learn mode. Pay attention to how they write to clients specifically: "we" or "I", how formal, how they open and sign off.

## 7.3 Fill in the notes

| File | What goes in |
|---|---|
| `context/business.md` | What the business does, for whom, location, website, their story in 2 lines, and their proof: reviews, results, well-known clients (only ones they're happy to name) |
| `context/people.md` | Team and helpers, key clients, and how formal to be with each |
| `context/priorities.md` | Ask one question: "What matters most over the next 3 months?" (It tells you which work they want more of.) |
| `context/routines.md` | Working days and hours |
| `context/voice.md` | From 7.2 |

Change each file's status line to `Status: filled in on <date>`.

## 7.4 Check it with them

Show a 4 line summary and ask what's wrong:

> Here's what I've got. Fern & Finch is a 2 person branding and web studio in Bristol, mostly local hospitality, healthcare and property clients. Your proposals should be friendly and plain, in "I", signed "Cheers, Sam". You'd like more website work this quarter. Anything I've got wrong?

Fix what they correct. Tick step 7.
