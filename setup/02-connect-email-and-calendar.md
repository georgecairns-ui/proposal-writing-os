# Step 5: connect email (and calendar)

Goal: you can read their email, so you can see what each client asked for and **confirmed in writing** (budget, scope, dates, who decides), and spot new requests for a proposal or a quote. The calendar is a bonus: it shows which clients they're meeting.

## 5.1 One sentence on why

> This is the big one. Clients confirm the important things by email, so when I write a proposal I'll read your emails with that client and use exactly what they agreed. I never send, reply to or delete anything.

If they'd rather not connect email, note it in `context/tools.md` and move on. Proposals still work from what's in each client's folder.

## 5.2 Connect

Follow the guide for their provider, exactly:

| They use | Follow |
|---|---|
| Gmail or Google Workspace, Google Calendar | `setup/connections/gmail-and-google-calendar.md` |
| Outlook or Microsoft 365 with a work account | `setup/connections/microsoft-365.md` |
| Outlook.com, Hotmail, iCloud, Yahoo or another provider | Read the "If neither works" section of `setup/connections/microsoft-365.md`, then offer: forward work email to a Gmail account they connect, or skip email for now and save client emails into their folders by hand |

Menus in the Claude app change. Describe what to look for, ask what they can see, and adapt. Never ask for their email password; they sign in on Google's or Microsoft's own screen.

## 5.3 Check it works

With their permission, read the subjects and senders of the 5 most recent emails, and say what you see in one line, without quoting anything private:

> I can see your inbox (last email from Dev at Hollins Lettings, 20 minutes ago).

If it fails, try once more, then note it in `setup/progress.md` and carry on. It can be fixed later.

## 5.4 Record it

Add a row for each to `context/tools.md` ("Connected apps"): app, how connected, today's date, "Read; save summaries into client folders", and "Never send, reply, forward, delete, archive, label or unsubscribe". Tick step 5 in `setup/progress.md`.

## Rules for their inbox, from now on

- Read only what a proposal needs: emails with that client. Don't read more than you need.
- Never send, reply, forward, delete, archive, label, mark as read, unsubscribe or accept an invite, even though the connection technically allows some of these.
- The covering email and follow-ups live on the proposal in the app; the person opens them in their email and sends them. Save a draft into their mailbox only if they ask for that specific email.
- Don't copy whole private emails into this folder. A short summary with the key facts and exact words that matter, in the client's folder, is enough.
