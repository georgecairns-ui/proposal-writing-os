# Marketing Proposal Writing OS

**In one sentence:** put everything about a client in one folder, press one button, and get a proposal in your own brand with your own prices, ready to check and send.

## Before and after

**Before.** A proposal takes an evening (to check). You open the last one, save it as a new file, change the name in 4 places (and miss the fifth), rewrite the bits that don't fit, and guess at a price you quoted differently last time. The client's actual words from the call are in a notebook somewhere.

**After.** Notes, the call and their emails go in the client's folder. You press Write proposal. A few minutes later (to check) there's a proposal in your brand that opens with what they told you was hurting, in their words, offers your real packages at your real prices with one recommended, and ends with how you'll get started. You change a line or 2, save the PDF and send it.

Time-saving claims are marked "to check": test them on real businesses before using a number.

## The 3 things to show in a 60 second video

1. **The folder and the button.** Drag a call transcript and an email into "Hollins Lettings", open the app, press Write proposal. Cut to it appearing.
2. **The document.** Scroll the proposal: their logo on a green cover, the pain in the client's own words, 2 options with "Recommended" on one and prices worked out with VAT. Click a sentence and change it.
3. **Save as PDF and send.** Save it as a PDF, open the covering email Claude wrote, attach, done. End on "Your brand. Your prices. Your words. Checked by you."
