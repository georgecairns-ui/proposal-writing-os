# Skills

Saved know-how for writing your proposals. You don't need to remember any of this: ask in your own words and Claude picks the right one.

Each skill is a folder with a `SKILL.md` inside. Claude reads them from here whenever this folder is open. During setup, Claude also writes you skills of your own, above all your own proposal skill, named after your business. They're saved here, and packed up in the `skills-to-upload` folder so you can add them to Claude and use them in every conversation.

## Your proposals

In Claude Code, every skill is also a command: type `/` and its name, for example `/write-a-proposal`.

| Skill | Say something like | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| `write-a-proposal` | Press **Write proposal**, or "write a proposal for Hollins" | Claude reads everything about the client (their folder, your emails and calls with them) and writes the whole proposal in your brand, with your packages and prices |
| `check-client-folders` | Press **Check for new folders**, or "I've added a client" | New client folders show in the app, with what's in them and whether there's enough to write a proposal. Claude writes the new ones if you've switched that on |
| `change-a-proposal` | **Ask Claude** on a proposal, or "make it shorter" | Just that change, without touching your own edits |
| `follow-up-a-proposal` | "Chase Marlow", "who hasn't replied?" | A friendly follow-up for proposals gone quiet, with a real reason to reply |
| `import-a-proposal-template` | "Here's our template" | Claude copies your proposal's order, headings, standard wording and look |
| `build-my-proposal-skill` | "Rebuild my proposal skill" | Your own skill, with your packages, prices, template and tone, brought up to date and packed up to add to Claude |
| `proposals-check-in` | "Check my proposals" (runs a few times a day, at times you agreed) | New client emails and calls saved into their folders, new clients picked up, waiting proposals written, follow-ups drafted |
| `sort-brain-dump` | Press **Brain dump** on any page (or V) and talk | Claude sorts it and sends each part to the right tool |
| `sort-proposals-brain-dump` | (used by `sort-brain-dump`) | The proposals part: notes filed in client folders, proposals started or changed |

## Writing

| Skill | Say something like | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| `human-email` | "Make this sound less robotic" | Any draft is checked so it reads like a person wrote it. Used by every writing skill |
| `write-like-me` | "Learn how I write" | Claude learns your style (with permission) and writes in it |
| `polite-chaser` | "Chase the Greenway invoice" | A chaser at the right level of firmness, with all the specifics |

## Keeping things organised

| Skill | Say something like | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| `file-it` | "Save this" | Claude suggests a folder and a name, and files it once you agree. Anything about a client goes in their folder |
| `connect-a-tool` | "Connect my Gmail" | Claude walks you through connecting an app safely |
| `match-my-brand` | "Make the app match my brand" | Your colours, font and logo on the app. For your proposals' look, say "make my proposals match my brand" |
| `setup` | `/setup` | Runs the setup, or carries on where it left off |
| `open-the-app` | "Open the app", "Open my proposals" | Starts the app if needed and gives you the link, http://localhost:4747 |
| `get-updates` | "Get the latest version", "any updates?" | Your copy brought up to date with a safe backup first. Your records, notes, skills and brand stay exactly as they are |
| `keep-context-fresh` | (automatic) | When you mention a new service, price or way of working, Claude offers to update its notes and your proposal skill |

## The rules every skill follows

- Claude writes; you check, approve and send. Claude never sends anything.
- Every price comes from your own packages. Nothing is made up: anything nobody said is listed for you to fill in.
- Claude never writes legal terms. Only your own, word for word.
- Everything stays in this folder or in the apps you've connected.

## Adding your own

Ask Claude: "Make a skill for how we do X." It'll write one in this folder using the same format, and show it to you first.
